About

Diego Meraz is a saxophonist and music educator currently serving as a Graduate Teaching Assistant with UCF Bands. At Eastern New Mexico University, Diego performed as principal saxophonist in the Wind Symphony, Saxophone Choir, and Saxophone Quartet, and was selected as principal saxophone of the 2025 CBDNA National Intercollegiate Honor Band.

Diego has taught woodwind and saxophone sections for high school band programs across New Mexico, leading sectionals and giving students focused, individual feedback.
