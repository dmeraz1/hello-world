A Program Notes

Diego Meraz is a saxophonist and music educator currently serving as a Graduate Teaching Assistant with the University of Central Florida Bands. A 2025 Music Education graduate of Eastern New Mexico University, Diego led the Greyhound Sound Marching Band as drum major, performed as principal saxophonist in ENMU's premier ensembles, and was selected as principal saxophone of the 2025 CBDNA National Intercollegiate Honor Band.

Summers are spent with high school marching programs across New Mexico — running woodwind and saxophone sectionals, teaching marching fundamentals, and helping students sound and move their best.
