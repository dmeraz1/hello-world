👋 Welcome, musician!

Learn to read ahead, one tap at a time.

Sight reading gets easier when your eyes stay a step ahead of your fingers. Pick some music, tap along with the rhythm, and Sightline shows you exactly where you got stuck, so next time you can see it coming.

Choose your music

Start with a practice piece, or bring the part you're working on in band.

To load your own part, export a single clarinet part from MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, or Noteflight as uncompressed MusicXML (.musicxml or .xml). Parts in 4/4, up to 16 measures.

2

Change the key or the rhythms

Same piece, new challenge. Great for when you've memorized it!

Keys are written for B♭ clarinet. New rhythms keep the notes of the piece and change only the rhythm.

3

Tap Along

Press Start, listen to the 4-beat count-in, then tap once for every note. Measures disappear as you go, so keep your eyes moving ahead!

Where you got stuck

    Try this next

    Sightline adjusts after every run. As you get more notes on time, measures disappear sooner and reminders come later, until you're reading ahead on your own. Having a tough day? It eases back.

    💡 Stuck on the same spot? Check the sight-reading tips.

    How Sightline helps

    👀

    Disappearing measures

    Each measure vanishes while you're still playing it, so you have to read the next one ahead of time. That's the secret skill of great sight readers.

    ⚠️

    Heads-up reminders

    Dotted rhythms, syncopation, and sixteenths are spotted automatically and highlighted. A reminder pops up a couple of beats before each one.

    👏

    Tap Along

    Tapping the rhythm lets you practice reading anywhere, even without your instrument. Your report shows each note you were late on or missed.
